MUNIN-1

STUDENT ONBOARDING AND SYSTEMS ENGINEERING PROGRAM

MUNIN-1 is our foundational onboarding project, created to introduce new members to space systems engineering, teamwork, and hands-on technical development. The current cohort is developing a weather-balloon payload scheduled to launch Feb–Mar 2026 from Andøya Space — the project is open to all and requires no prior experience.

Project Overview

Goal

Build the technical foundation for new members through a structured and collaborative learning experience.

Core focus

Members learn the fundamentals of engineering design, project workflows, documentation, and systems-thinking.

Learning model

The project supports a practical, team-driven method of teaching, where students gain experience by contributing to real engineering work.

Mission Objectives

Skills

Introduce students to essential engineering skills, including design, testing, and iteration.

Teamwork

Build interdisciplinary collaboration between students from different technical backgrounds and interests.

Leadership

Prepare members for future project roles and more advanced technical responsibilities within Polarix.

Teams

Mechanical

CAD modelling, 3D printing and assembly of payload structures.

Electrical

PCB design, manufacturing and soldering for onboard electronics.

Software

Microcontroller programming, sensor integration and data handling.

Systems

System design, simulation, mission planning, documentation and requirements.
