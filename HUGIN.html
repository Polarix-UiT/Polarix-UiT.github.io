HUGIN

ROVER PLATFORM FOR LUNAR ASSISTED EXPLORATION

Hugin is Polarix UiT’s rover-focused project for the LOOK Competition, designed to explore terrain navigation, autonomy, and mission systems in space. We are currently in the second part of the competition(LOOK27).

Project Gallery

Project Overview

Mission

Develop a capable rover platform for competition and mission-oriented engineering, with a focus on mobility, sensing, and autonomous operation.

Core focus

Hugin is built around practical systems engineering: drivetrain design, payload integration, navigation, and robust testing.

Collaboration

The project gives students hands-on experience in rover design while connecting them with wider space-innovation and engineering communities.

Mission Objectives

Autonomy

Develop navigation and path-planning strategies that improve the rover’s ability to handle uncertain terrain.

Mobility

Design a robust drivetrain and suspension layout optimized for rough, uneven surfaces and mission constraints.

Testing

Validate system performance through iterative prototyping, field trials, and mission simulations.
